# Kairos AR

> Kairos AR, Inc. is a human-analytics company providing face recognition, facial detection, and emotion analysis as a REST API. Enroll, verify, and recognize faces against galleries you create; detect faces and facial attributes (age, gender, ethnicity, glasses, landmarks); and analyze emotion, demographics, and attention across images and video. JSON in/out, authenticated with an app_id + app_key header pair.

## APIs

- [Kairos Face Recognition & Emotion Analysis API](https://face.kairos.com/docs/api/): REST API over https://api.kairos.com — enroll, verify, recognize, detect, gallery management, and /v2 emotion analysis.
